# 🛵 Instafood: OPERATIONAL LOGISTICS & PLATFORM PERFORMANCE ANALYTICS

## Executive Summary

Instafood provides its services in the major Indian metropolitan areas (Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi, Hyderabad, and Chennai). Although order volumes in the top figures are still strong in the 10,000 core transactions, the platform's growth is hindered by an operational bottleneck since 1,047 orders (10.47% of the total platform volume) were either not fulfilled or cancelled, thus losing ₹3,30,680 in gross revenue (10.24% of the gross platform GMV).

This incident does not represent a systemic failure across the entire platform, but rather obeys the **Pareto Principle (the 80/20 rule)** i.e. the operational friction is largely concentrated among a small number of high-volume partner kitchens.

* **Top 10 Underperforming Partners (14% of restaurants):** Of the 71 partner restaurants, only 10 sites are responsible for ₹1,50,512 (45.52% of all lost revenue) as a result of kitchen congestion during peak dining hours.

**High-density bottlenecks:** Places such as *Mahesh Lunch Home* (with 57 orders not fulfilled and a loss of ₹17,472) and *Bademiya* (with 54 orders not fulfilled and a loss of ₹17,261) have the highest number of order drop-offs across the platform.

* **Customer Retention Risk:** This local inconvenience has a direct adverse effect on the user experience and is the reason why **37.5% of the 2023 active customer accounts placed no orders in 2024**.

By surgically eliminating the operational friction at these 10 key partner restaurants, almost half of the platform's lost revenue will be regained instantly.

---

## Actionable Recommendations & Implementation Plan

* Install the auto-throttling procedure for the top 10 partners: when the active kitchen preparation queues exceed the threshold limits at high-risk locations such as Mahesh Lunch Home and Bademiya, automated pauses should be put in place for order acceptance.

* **Shift-Based Fleet Redistribution:** During the peak meal periods (i.e., between 2:00 PM and 4:00 PM and between 7:00 PM and 10:00 PM) reallocate the number of riders so that the average turnaround time is reduced from 40 minutes to near 25 minutes.

* For VIP accounts that became inactive in 2024, launch targeted re-engagement offers featuring the top-selling items in each region (for example, Chicken Biryani in Bengaluru and Paneer Butter Masala in Mumbai) in order to keep them.
